egg-picker · assembly manual (r003)

8 steps · 138 min · tools: 2 mm hex key, 2.5 mm hex key, USB-C cable, flush cutters, soldering iron + insert tip

1. Base plate, base actuator and electronics

Press the eight M3 inserts into the base plate at 220 °C. Seat the STS3215 (ID 1) with its spline up and fix it with four M3×8. Mount the controller PCBA on M2.5 standoffs with the USB-C port facing the rear cut-out.

⏱ 24 min · soldering iron + insert tip, 2.5 mm hex key, 2 mm hex key

  • 4× M3×8 SHCS @ 0.8 N·m
  • 8× M3 heat-set insert
  • 4× M2.5×6 standoff screw @ 0.4 N·m

2. Turret

Drop the lazy-susan bearing over the base boss, then bolt the turret shell to the J1 horn at 0° (index mark forward).

⏱ 12 min · 2 mm hex key, 2.5 mm hex key

  • 4× M2.5×6 horn screw @ 0.4 N·m
  • 4× M3×8 SHCS @ 0.8 N·m

3. Shoulder

Fit the J2 servo into the turret cradle. Attach the upper-arm link to the horn with the arm vertical (servo at 2048 ticks).

⏱ 18 min · 2.5 mm hex key, 2 mm hex key

  • 6× M3×8 SHCS @ 0.8 N·m
  • 4× M2.5×6 horn screw @ 0.4 N·m

4. Upper arm + elbow

Mount J3 at the top of the upper arm and attach the forearm link straight up. Run the bus cable along the link channel.

⏱ 16 min · 2.5 mm hex key, 2 mm hex key

  • 6× M3×8 SHCS @ 0.8 N·m
  • 4× M2.5×6 horn screw @ 0.4 N·m

5. Forearm + wrist

Install the J4 wrist-pitch servo and the wrist link. Check ±108° of travel without cable snag.

⏱ 14 min · 2.5 mm hex key

  • 4× M3×8 SHCS @ 0.8 N·m
  • 4× M2.5×6 horn screw @ 0.4 N·m

6. Bus wiring + servo IDs

Daisy-chain W1-W6 from the PCBA bus port through each servo. Connect USB-C and run `morph flash ids` to assign IDs 1-6 one servo at a time.

⏱ 20 min · flush cutters, USB-C cable

  • 8× cable tie 100 mm

7. Gripper body

Mount the gripper servo (ID 6) to the gripper plate, press the pinion onto its spline and slide both racks in from the sides.

⏱ 15 min · 2.5 mm hex key, 2 mm hex key

  • 4× M3×8 SHCS @ 0.8 N·m
  • 2× M2.5×6 horn screw @ 0.4 N·m

8. Fingers and sensor pads

Seat each FSR 402 in its finger pocket (tail through the slot), slide the pad onto the dovetail, then bolt the fingers to the racks with the pads touching a 44 mm gauge.

⏱ 19 min · 2.5 mm hex key

  • 4× M3×16 SHCS @ 0.6 N·m
